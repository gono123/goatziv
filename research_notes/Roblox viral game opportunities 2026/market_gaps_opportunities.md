## 1. Which 2025–2026 Steam/indie/mobile hits have or have not had a dominant Roblox equivalent?

### Takeaway
Roblox clones a viral Steam co-op or social game within days or weeks, and the clones often out-draw the original in raw players. Examples: Meccha Chameleon, Librarian, Peak, Lethal Company, R.E.P.O. So the "friendslop" co-op lane isn't empty. It's a speed race won by whoever ships first, with backlash risk. The less-copied lanes are deeper-systems genres: roguelite deckbuilders (Balatro-likes), Deep Rock Galactic-style co-op PvE, polished extraction shooters, and crime/management sims like Schedule I. The searches found no dominant Roblox equivalent for any of these. Supermarket and Gas Station sims do have established Roblox versions.

### Inferences
- In co-op "friendslop" and social deduction, the gap isn't missing games. It's quality and timing. Clones win by being first, free and SEO-matched, and many are visibly low effort. That leaves two angles. (a) A fast follower with genuinely better execution. (b) An original twist on the proven mechanic (proximity voice, physics, mimicry) that avoids copying IP. The PEAK/CLIFF backlash and reported takedown show that direct copying carries reputational and IP risk.
- The Meccha Chameleon cycle (Steam hit → Roblox clones with more CCU inside ~3 weeks) suggests a repeatable playbook: watch the Steam top-sellers and Twitch/TikTok co-op virality, then ship a Roblox-native take within 2–4 weeks.
- Systems-heavy indie genres (deckbuilders, roguelites, DRG-style class co-op, crime/management sims) look underbuilt on Roblox. The likely reason is that they take more design work than a one-mechanic clone. That fits the "everything is an anime bandit beater" complaint.
- Schedule I's drug-dealing theme probably conflicts with Roblox's content rules. That inference is mine and isn't sourced here (see Gaps). A re-themed version (a "food truck empire" or "potion dealer" sim with the same expand-the-operation loop) may be the workable form.

### Gaps
- No verified current CCU figures for any Roblox R.E.P.O. clone, Chained Together-like, Blue Prince-like or mimic game. RoMonitor and Rolimons data were not reachable.
- Whether any Roblox deckbuilder or roguelite has meaningful CCU is unverified. Searching roblox.com directly was not possible in this session.
- Could not confirm the takedown status of CLIFF, or Roblox's current policy on clone IP complaints.
- Roblox Community Standards on depicting drugs were not checked in this session.

## 2. What do Roblox players request ("I wish there was a Roblox game where...")?

### Takeaway
Direct Reddit, DevForum and TikTok evidence couldn't be retrieved. Searches aimed at r/roblox and "I wish there was a Roblox game" returned no threads. The proxy signals that did come back point the same way. Older and lapsed players want realistic, higher-fidelity shooters and action. Players are tired of anime fighting and simulator sameness. People want genres they love elsewhere, such as DRG-style co-op and Plants vs. Zombies-style tower defense.

### Cited Findings
- In Newzoo's survey of 167 Americans aged 5–35 who stopped playing Roblox, 46% now play Fortnite or Minecraft (29% and 28% individually), 26% play GTA and 30% moved to Call of Duty. Coverage describes these players as preferring "realistic shooters such as Call of Duty and GTA Online." The sample is small. — [GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/6631-report-explores-how-roblox-can-maintain-older-players-as-it-becomes-most-played/); [daily.dev](https://daily.dev/posts/report-explores-how-roblox-can-maintain-older-players-as-it-becomes-most-played-game-by-total-hours--18a2xjkgs); [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/roblox-older-players-4-05-billion-hours-newzoo-2026/)
- The report suggests building around recognizable entertainment IP and testing action-heavy designs borrowed from realistic shooters. — [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/roblox-older-players-4-05-billion-hours-newzoo-2026/)
- In a Steam community thread, players said "nobody has made a DRG clone on roblox yet," complained "everything is an anime bandit beater on roblox," and asked for more Plants vs. Zombies-like games. — [Steam Community](https://steamcommunity.com/app/548430/discussions/1/3801650561761330031)
- Roblox announced "Roblox Reality" in April 2026 to enable more realistic projects, partly in response to older players' preferences. — [daily.dev summary of Newzoo/GEEIQ](https://daily.dev/posts/report-explores-how-roblox-can-maintain-older-players-as-it-becomes-most-played-game-by-total-hours--18a2xjkgs); [TechCrunch RDC 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/roblox-is-making-it-easier-to-build-games-with-ai-and-play-them-outside-roblox/)

### Inferences
- "Realistic shooter or action for 16+" is the clearest stated demand signal backed by survey data, and Roblox Reality, the realism push, lowers the technical barrier. A polished, realistic-looking co-op PvE or extraction shooter fits both the request and the platform direction.
- The anime-fighter fatigue comment is anecdotal but matches the vendor claim (Q4) that demand exceeds quality supply in non-simulator genres.

### Gaps
- No primary Reddit, DevForum or TikTok "I wish there was" threads were retrieved. Search returned none, and Reddit could not be fetched directly. A report writer should not claim specific Reddit requests from these notes.
- No TikTok comment analysis was available.

## 3. Which audience segments are underserved?

### Takeaway
Three segments stand out as large and under-targeted: (1) players aged 16+/18+, now about 27% of age-verified users and big spenders, (2) girls and women, where Dress to Impress showed latent demand, and (3) fast-growing low-ARPU regions, mainly India, plus Japan and Indonesia. New age tiers (Roblox Kids, Select, standard 16+) and 18+ "Restricted" experiences now formally split the audience, which creates room for games aimed only at older players.

### Cited Findings
**Older players (16+/18+)**
- Among age-verified users (Q1 2026), 35% are under 13, 38% are 13–17 and 27% are 18+. — [GEEIQ](https://geeiq.com/resources/blog/how-to-market-to-gen-z-and-gen-alpha-in-2026); consistent with the [Roblox Q4 2025 Shareholder Letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Q4-2025-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- 80% of Roblox players over 16 spent money on games in the past six months, against 59% of non-Roblox players. — [daily.dev / Newzoo-GEEIQ](https://daily.dev/posts/report-explores-how-roblox-can-maintain-older-players-as-it-becomes-most-played-game-by-total-hours--18a2xjkgs)
- Roblox logged ~4.05B hours on PC and console in H1 2026, the most-played game by total hours, ahead of Fortnite (2.89B, −26% YoY) and Minecraft (2.28B, −10% YoY). — [GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/6631-report-explores-how-roblox-can-maintain-older-players-as-it-becomes-most-played/); [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-52471)
- Account tiers went global on 16 June 2026: Roblox Kids (5–8), Roblox Select (9–15) and standard Roblox (16+). Another source says age verification has gated social hangouts since May 2026, and that age verification has run worldwide since January 2026 with six age groups. — [GEEIQ](https://geeiq.com/resources/blog/how-to-market-to-gen-z-and-gen-alpha-in-2026); [GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-and-geeiq-the-roblox-audience)
- "Restricted" experiences replaced the 17+ label in late 2025. Access is moving to 18+ only, and creators must be 18+ to publish Restricted experiences. Restricted experiences may include stronger violence, romance, crude humor and depictions of gambling and alcohol. At the 2023 launch of the 17+ category, those experiences could not use Immersive Ads, User Ads or Sponsored Experiences. — [DevForum: Restricted age requirements](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/updating-age-requirements-for-experiences-with-%E2%80%98restricted%E2%80%99-content-maturity-label/3905863); [Roblox Newsroom Sept 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/09/roblox-to-expand-age-estimation-to-all-users); [TechCrunch 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/20/roblox-invites-its-community-to-build-mature-experiences-for-17-users)
- I found no data on how 17+ or Restricted experiences actually perform in CCU or revenue. — (search result; no source available)

**Girls and women**
- Dress to Impress has over 11 billion visits and a team of ~30 developers (2025). A Substack analysis frames dress-up as a neglected niche. — [Spawn Point Marketing](https://spawnpointmarketing.substack.com/p/too-niche-to-fail-how-dressmaker); [Wikipedia: Dress to Impress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dress_to_Impress_(video_game))
- About half of Dress to Impress users are older than 18. — [Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/business/technology/roblox-for-adults-dress-to-impress-draws-a-crowd/)
- The developer of Dressmaker (Steam: 17,000 reviews, 98% positive within a month) says "women are such an underserved market" and notes a "surprisingly large population of women, not just young girls" on platforms like Roblox playing dress-up games. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/women-love-video-games-dressmaker-dev-says-there-just-arent-enough-games-for-them-we-were-definitely-targeting-women-from-the-get-go/)
- Female players are nostalgic for gone-but-loved browser staples such as GirlsGoGames and Pixie Hollow. — [Gen Alpha Substack / search summary](https://genalpha.substack.com/p/how-robloxs-dress-to-impress-is-signaling)
- Sonic Speed Simulator's audience is 78% male, while the Sandbox genre's audience (mostly Roblox and Minecraft players) is evenly split by gender. — [GameDev Reports (Newzoo/GEEIQ)](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-and-geeiq-the-roblox-audience)

**Regions**
- Q3 2026: global DAU was 138M, down 9% YoY and up 12% QoQ. India DAU grew 37% YoY. Bookings are expected to fall 10–15% YoY in Q4, and monthly unique payers fell 16% to 30M. — [Outlook India Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-q3-2026-india-growth-amid-ai-push-and-falling-bookings)
- Q2 2026: DAU grew 67% in Japan and 64% in India. — [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics/)
- Q4 2025 (aggregator): Indonesia up over 700%, Japan up 160%, India up 110%, Korea up 120%. These are secondary figures. — [VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/roblox-statistics-2026/)
- TD Cowen alleged that bot activity inflated Grow a Garden, pointing to a surge in account creation in the Philippines and Indonesia. Roblox disputes this. — [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- A vendor guide says Indian players lean towards simulators and competitive games, and Brazilian players towards roleplay and social experiences. It argues that regional price cuts in low-purchasing-power markets drive the biggest revenue lifts. This is vendor modelling, not audited data. — [RoLearn regional pricing](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/regional-pricing-international-markets/)

### Inferences
- Older players are the clearest underserved segment that also pays. A large share of 18+ users, high spending propensity, documented churn to CoD and GTA, and a new 16+/18+ gating system together make a mature-skewing game (realistic co-op shooter, mature social deduction, horror) an evidence-backed bet. The trade-off: Restricted-labelled games lose under-18 reach and, at least at launch, some ad products.
- The female and older-female audience is proven (Dress to Impress) but served by very few hits. Adjacent concepts such as cozy creative social games, fashion and design competitions, home, café or boutique design-and-judge loops, and pet-styling look under-supplied relative to that proof point.
- India, Japan and Indonesia growth is mostly low-ARPU, so for a viral-first developer these regions are a CCU and discovery lever rather than a revenue lever. Localization, mobile-first controls and low-spec performance matter. The raised minimum draw distance on low-end devices (Q4) points to Roblox investing there too.

### Gaps
- No overall Roblox gender split was found in these searches.
- No data on Brazil- or Philippines-specific genre hits, and no breakdown of voice-chat usage.
- No performance data for Restricted or 17+ experiences.

## 4. Which new Roblox platform features enable game types that haven't been fully exploited?

### Takeaway
Late 2026 brings several new or expanding features: LLM-based Text Generation for NPC dialogue, AI NPC behaviour tools, prompt-to-game "Build", a Scene Generator, Roblox Reality for realistic visuals, browser and standalone app distribution, and Roblox Wallet payouts. No breakout game built around conversational AI NPCs turned up in the searches. That's a plausible white space, with the catch that games built around ongoing AI conversation reportedly need a Restricted (18+) label.

### Cited Findings
- RDC 2026 (September): AI to support NPC behaviour, including generating dialogue and text in response to player actions, plus NPCs that can playtest games (expected by year end). The "Build" tool lets users make games from natural-language prompts, reportedly whole games in 10–15 minutes. About 9,000 games have reportedly been published with Build. A Scene Generator, which turns a text prompt and reference image into a functional layout, is due later in 2026. Roblox Wallet adds automatic daily payouts in local currency. Browser play and standalone app publishing were also announced. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/roblox-is-making-it-easier-to-build-games-with-ai-and-play-them-outside-roblox/); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/rdc-2026); [Adgully](https://www.adgully.com/post/20490/roblox-unveils-standalone-publishing-and-ai-suite-at-rdc-2026); [DevForum RDC26 recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880)
- Caveat: Roblox Reality, AI-assisted NPC tools and standalone apps were announced without firm release windows. — [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/roblox-rdc-every-major-announcement-for-players-and-creators/)
- The Text Generation API uses LLMs to power dynamic NPC dialogue, and all output must pass through TextService:FilterStringAsync(). — [Roblox Creator Docs: AI](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ai/accelerated-workflows); [Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/updates/id/532634/article/roblox-implementing-beta-for-text-generation-ai/)
- AI interactions must be disclosed in the Content Maturity questionnaire, and games built around ongoing AI conversations need a Restricted label, which keeps them from under-18s. This comes from a 2026 third-party guide, not official policy text. — [PromptBlox](https://promptblox.ai/roblox-ai)
- Developers can call external LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic and others) through HttpService. — [KitsBlox](https://kitsblox.com/blog/roblox-ai-npcs-changing-development)
- A Naavik podcast guest said few games use AI chatbots or AI visual generation on top of Roblox, and called it an opportunity. — [Naavik podcast](https://naavik.co/podcast/building-ai-tools-for-roblox-creators/)
- The Fall 2026 Creator Roadmap added 72 items and listed 47 newly shipped features. Late-2026 items include Open Cloud APIs for AI tools, an Assistant NPC subagent, early testing with up to 10 age-checked friends, Android anti-cheat with hardware attestation, a minimum draw distance on low-end devices raised from 200 to 500 studs (third-party tracker), direct sprite sheet import, developer push notifications and Roblox Wallet. Roblox says 130 roadmap features launched since the previous RDC, 79% on time. — [Roblox on X](https://x.com/Roblox/status/2101062623479693705); [DevForum Fall Roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-fall-update/4880208); [BloxRant tracker](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-creator-roadmap); [Roblox Roadmap](https://create.roblox.com/roadmap)
- Cube 3D is Roblox's generative 3D model. — [Wikipedia: Cube 3D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cube_3D)
- Roblox widened its recommendation window from 7 to 28 days, which deliberately sends fewer players to the experiences that monetize hardest. Naavik frames this as accepting a weaker 2026 for a stronger 2028. — [Naavik, State of UGC Games 2026](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)

### Inferences
- **AI-NPC social deduction and interrogation:** detective or "find the AI impostor" games, and talk-your-way-out escape rooms, are made possible by the Text Generation API and seem largely unexploited. The Restricted-label requirement for ongoing AI conversation (if the third-party guide is accurate) points such games at 18+ players. That fits the underserved older segment, but it shrinks reach.
- **Realistic shooters and horror:** Roblox Reality plus 16+ tiers line up with the documented older-player demand. Early movers on realistic-fidelity co-op PvE or extraction could gain before the space crowds, but release timing is unclear.
- **Browser and standalone distribution** may favour games that go viral through link sharing (short-session party games), since players can join without the full app. This is speculative until the feature ships.
- The 28-day recommendation window reportedly rewards retention over short-term monetization. Concepts with long-term progression or social retention may get more algorithmic support than pure spike-and-fade clones.

### Gaps
- No named Roblox experience with high CCU built mainly on LLM NPCs was found.
- No sourced details on Moments (video feed), ads changes, voice chat usage or live-event tooling in 2026. These weren't covered in the searches.
- The official policy text on AI conversation requiring a Restricted label wasn't confirmed, only third-party summaries.

## 5. What do analysts cite as untapped opportunities?

### Takeaway
Analysts point to genre holes (4X/strategy and MOBA in Naavik's older piece), a shortage of new breakout hits, under-use of AI chat and visual generation, under-developed advertising, user acquisition and live-ops practice, and older players as the segment to retain. Vendor blogs (RoLearn) list Survival, Puzzle, Sports, Racing, Building/Sandbox and Educational/trivia as low-competition, but their methodology is proprietary and partly self-contradictory.

### Cited Findings
- Naavik's "Predicting the Next Big Hits on Roblox" argues that no real technical barrier blocks any genre, and that 4X/strategy and MOBAs have not yet broken out on Roblox. That piece is about 16 months old. — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/predicting-the-next-big-hits-on-roblox/)
- Naavik's 2026 digest says Roblox still has untapped potential in in-game advertising, user acquisition and live-ops expertise, and needs more breakout hits. — [Naavik, "Roblox's Expensive Year of Good Intentions"](https://naavik.co/digest/robloxs-expensive-year-of-good-intentions/)
- Naavik State of UGC 2026: Roblox paid developers $1.5B in 2025 (+70% YoY), out of ~$2.2B across Roblox, Fortnite and Overwolf (+47%). ### Inferences
- Evidence from independent analysts, survey data and community complaints converges on a few concept clusters with real viral potential that haven't been done well:
  1. **Systems-rich co-op PvE** (DRG-, R.E.P.O.- or Lethal-style, with class roles and proximity voice) at higher fidelity for 13+/16+. Clones exist but are often low effort, and DRG-style is reportedly absent.
  2. **Realistic co-op or extraction shooters** for 16+, matching the lapsed-to-CoD demand and the Roblox Reality push. Only small entries exist plus one unannounced high-profile project.
  3. **Roguelite deckbuilder or Balatro-like social variants.** No Roblox entries surfaced, though the genre is proven on Steam and mobile. A multiplayer or social twist (draft against friends, shared runs) would fit Roblox.
  4. **AI-NPC games** (interrogation, deduction, "convince the NPC") using the Text Generation API. Analysts call this under-used, with Restricted-label caveats.
  5. **Female and older-female creative-social games** beyond dress-up (design, judge, cozy competition). Dress to Impress proved demand, and few competitors are documented.
  6. **Strategy, 4X or MOBA**, flagged by Naavik as never having broken out. This is higher-risk and the source is dated.
- Vendor "unsaturated genre" lists shouldn't be treated as independent validation. RoLearn sells tools and its scores aren't public.
