## 2. Breakouts in the last 3–6 months (Jun–Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
2026's defining breakout is **Steal An Egg**. It launched 25 Jul 2026 and hit a ~14.3M all-time peak CCU on 19 Sep 2026, the highest single-game CCU recorded in 2026. It has passed ~10B visits, and its core loop is a near-copy of Steal a Brainrot's. The other big 2026 breakouts are **+1 Speed Keyboard Escape** (~6–7M peak, Jun–Aug 2026, now collapsed to ~70K) and a wave of "Steal a ___" clones such as Break and Steal an Egg. Engagement in Steal An Egg was already rolling over by early-to-mid October, according to Bank of America.

### Inferences
- The 2026 breakout pattern copies 2025's hits:
  - "Steal a ___" PvP-steal tycoons with collectible, rarity-tiered creatures (Steal An Egg, Break and Steal an Egg).
  - "+1 Speed" incremental obbies.
  - Brand concerts (bbno$, Jonas Brothers) used as CCU spike events.
- Fast clones (Break and Steal an Egg) can reach the top 5 within weeks of the original's success.
- Breakout lifecycles are short. +1 Speed Keyboard Escape fell ~99% in about three months. Steal An Egg peaked about eight weeks after launch and then declined, though it still holds about 1M live CCU.
- Steal An Egg's peak (~14.3M) is well below Steal a Brainrot's 25.4M and Grow a Garden's 22.3M (both 2025). 2026's biggest hit was therefore large but not record-breaking platform-wide.

## 4. Record-breaking CCU events in 2026 and Halloween 2026 spikes

### Takeaway
I found **no evidence of a new platform-wide CCU record in 2026**. The record is still ~45–47.4M, set on 23 Aug 2025. The 2026 per-game records are Steal An Egg's ~14.3M peak (19 Sep 2026), the year's highest, and +1 Speed Keyboard Escape's ~6–7M (Jun–Aug 2026). Halloween 2026 was driven by per-game events plus Roblox's "The Hollow" (22 Oct to 2 Nov), with MM2 and 99 Nights posting the largest 2M+ daily peaks.

### Cited Findings
