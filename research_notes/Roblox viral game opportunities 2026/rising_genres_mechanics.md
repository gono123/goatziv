## 1. Genre waves of 2025–2026: what is cresting, rising, or dead?

### Takeaway
2025 was a "mega-viral" year driven by Grow a Garden (idle/grow with offline progression) and Steal a Brainrot (steal/collect with Italian-brainrot memes). By 2026 both are well past peak, and the dominant wave has turned into "Steal a ___" derivatives (Steal an Egg was #1 by visits in Q3 2026), alongside rising co-op horror (99 Nights in the Forest), anime/fighting hybrids, and very short-session "+1 Speed/escape" games. In Nov 2026 the newest breakouts (DONT SPLASH, egg-themed clones) show the hit cycle is shorter and more clone-driven than ever. Basic simulators look saturated, while horror-social and genre hybrids look comparatively early.

## 2. What mechanics are hit games using, and why do they work?

### Takeaway
The 2025–2026 hits are built on a recognizable kit. Live-ops spectacle (admin-abuse events, scheduled weekly drops) creates appointment play and influencer moments. Scarce randomized stock (restocks with tiny odds, mutations) drives FOMO, trading and rarity status. Social friction (stealing from other players' bases) adds conflict and revenge loops, and offline/passive progression brings players back. Documentation of exact mechanics was thin in this session's sources, but several are confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **Admin abuse events:** The August 2025 "Admin Abuse War" between Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot set a platform concurrency record. — [Naavik State of UGC 2026](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/) (search summary)
- **Scheduled weekly events:** New Grow a Garden events drop on Saturday mornings, which drives concurrency spikes. — search summary over [GameDaily](https://gamedaily.com/games/everything-about-grow-a-garden-the-cozy-idle-sim-revolutionizing-roblox-revenue) / [MaxPower Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/inside-the-monetization-playbook-of-roblox-s-biggest-hit) / [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/dj4lxef8r) (exact attribution unclear)
- **Stock/restock timers with tiny odds:** A trading guide describes a Grow a Garden-style seed ("Moongrain") that first appeared in a shop with a 0.14% restock chance (Aug 2026). — [Bloxtrade](https://bloxtrade.org/articles/roblox-earnings-harvest-moon-moongrain-job-resume-trading-august-3-5-2026)
- **Randomized drops / loot-box criticism:** Critics argue that Grow a Garden's randomized seed drops approach "loot box" territory. — search summary over [GameDaily](https://gamedaily.com/games/everything-about-grow-a-garden-the-cozy-idle-sim-revolutionizing-roblox-revenue) / [MaxPower Gaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/inside-the-monetization-playbook-of-roblox-s-biggest-hit)
- **Mutations:** In Fisch, each fish can carry a mutation that changes its appearance and value. — [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/best-roblox-games-to-play-in-2026/)
- **Offline/passive growth:** Gardens grow offline as a passive progression design. — search summary over Grow a Garden analyses ([GameDaily](https://gamedaily.com/games/everything-about-grow-a-garden-the-cozy-idle-sim-revolutionizing-roblox-revenue))
- **Trading and real-money markets:** A thriving underground economy exists where players trade rare Grow a Garden items for real money, which violates Roblox's rules. — search summary ([France24](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20250626-roblox-s-grow-a-garden-explodes-online-video-game-numbers) / related)
- **Influencer/creator amplification:** YouTube/TikTok influencer videos drove much of the 2025 growth. — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)
- **Chat/voice as social glue:** After age checks, DAUs who sent a chat message or connected to voice grew 24% QoQ in Q3 2026, with 1.6B in-game chat messages/day (+13% QoQ). — [Q3 2026 transcript (Motley Fool)](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/roblox-rblx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) (search summary). Roblox advised creators to "increase the weight of the TextChat signal" in experiments if chat matters to their game. — [DevForum: Age Check Requirement Live Globally](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/age-check-requirement-to-chat-now-live-globally/4226101/4076)
- **AI-built content:** Two near-fully-AI-built games hit the top 20 by CCU in Q3 2026. — [Yahoo Q3 transcript](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/RBLX/earnings/RBLX-Q3-2026-earnings_call-720214.html). PPC Land reports that 80% of top games use AI and that developers are buying ads for traffic. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/roblox-developers-buy-ads-for-traffic-as-80-of-top-games-use-ai/)

### Inferences
- **Why they work:**
  - *Retention:* offline growth and restock timers create return triggers.
  - *Social loops:* stealing, trading and server-wide admin events give players stories to share and reasons to recruit friends.
  - *Monetization:* scarcity plus randomness turns dev products, such as restock skips, luck boosts and rare seeds, into impulse buys.
  - *Discovery:* admin-abuse and weekly events create CCU spikes and YouTube content on a predictable schedule.
- The 2026 algorithm favors 28-day+ retention (see §3). That rewards mechanics with long-tail return hooks (offline progression, collections, social bases) over pure one-week novelty.
- Background (unverified this session): Grow a Garden's weather events (rain, thunderstorms, "blood moon") apply mutations to crops. Steal a Brainrot's core loop is buying conveyor-belt brainrots and stealing others' from their bases, with timed base locks. 99 Nights is a 1–5 player co-op survival with a night counter. These match the mechanic patterns above but were not re-sourced here.

- No primary design breakdowns (dev interviews/YouTube postmortems) were retrievable. Exact restock cadences, weather systems and steal/lock mechanics were not sourced in 2026.

## 3. How has the Roblox platform changed in 2025–2026, and what does that shift?

### Takeaway
The biggest 2026 shifts are:
1. **A discovery algorithm (from Q2 2026) that deliberately gives more impressions to highly retentive games over fast monetizers.** At RDC 2026 Roblox said it now looks past the first week to the first 28 days, weighs younger and older cohorts, and tests "direct growth" (new players a game brings to the platform).
2. **Mandatory age checks for chat (global from Jan 7, 2026).** These segment social play by age group. They initially depressed social discovery, but chat activity was rising again by Q3.
3. **Creator Rewards (replacing engagement-based payouts, July 2025) and expanding rewarded video ads,** which add non-Robux income.
4. **RDC 2026 (Sept 2026):** Roblox Wallet with daily real-currency payouts, standalone app publishing, browser play and offline modes, and ads launchable from phones.

### Cited Findings
**Discovery algorithm**
- Changes to "Recommended for You" intentionally give highly retentive games more impressions, at the expense of near-term monetization. The near-term impact on younger cohorts was larger than expected. Monetization per hour came in below forecast, especially for under-13 users. — [Roblox Q2 2026 shareholder letter (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm); [GuruFocus Q2 2026 highlights](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8993610/roblox-corp-rblx-q2-2026-earnings-call-highlights-revenue-surges-36-to-15b-but-q3-bookings-forecast-signals-sharp-decline)
- The Q2 2026 shortfall reflected "a bigger-than-expected move of engagement from high-monetizing 2025 viral games to new and evergreen games with lower hourly monetization." — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm) (search summary)

### Inferences
- The retention-first algorithm plus the "28 days and beyond" evaluation window structurally disfavors pure flash-in-the-pan clones and favors games with long-tail loops. This may explain why 2026 hits peak lower and faster than Grow a Garden while evergreen titles gain share.
- "Direct growth" testing means games that pull new users onto Roblox (via TikTok/YouTube virality) may get an algorithmic bonus. That keeps influencer-bait design valuable.
- Age-gated chat raises the value of mechanics that work without chat (emotes, pings, visible trading UI, base-raiding), and of designs that drive friend invites (Trusted Connections).
- With US/Canada DAU and payers declining and India/Japan growing fast, low-ARPU, high-volume regions grow in importance. Rewarded video and Creator Rewards partly offset weak per-player spend.

### Gaps
- No sources found on "Today's Picks", "Commerce APIs", "Roblox Moments"/the short-video feed, or Cube/4D generation specifically in this session. Their 2026 status and effect on hit-making are unverified.
- No developer-side quantification of how the age-check rollout changed CCU for social/roleplay games.

## 4. What are monetization norms for top games now?

### Takeaway
Top 2025 hits monetized mainly through Robux dev products tied to randomness and scarcity (rare seeds, boosts, restocks, luck), plus gamepasses for automation and convenience, and cosmetics. ### Inferences
- Opportunity read for late 2026:
  - Avoid launching another plain "Steal a ___" or egg clone. The format is saturated and decaying within weeks.
  - Instead, pair a proven viral hook (meme IP, stealing, scarce restocks) with a retention spine (co-op progression, collections, offline growth) that scores on the 28-day+ algorithm.
  - Horror co-op and fighting hybrids look less crowded.
  - Lean on influencer-friendly live events (admin abuse, weekly drops).
  - Design for low-ARPU growth markets (India, Japan) with rewarded video.
