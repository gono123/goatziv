How a film gets made

  1. titles/batch-01.md pitches it; scripts/<slug>.md holds the narration.
  2. lines.js splits it into spoken lines per scene, with speaker prefixes.
  3. timing.js voices each line with engine/audio/voice.js and writes timeline.js.
  4. score.js places scenes, events and music from those measured times.
  5. song.js synthesises the soundtrack; film.js draws every scene.
  6. engine/render.js renders in headless Chrome, muxes, checks and verifies.
  7. youtube/<slug>.md is the upload kit for the finished film.
